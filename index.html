01 · The big picture

What is Jainism?

Jainism is an ancient Indian religious and philosophical tradition centered on liberation of the soul, careful conduct and responsibility for action.

🪷

Ahimsa

Ahimsa means non-violence and respect for living beings. It is one of the clearest signatures of Jain ethics.

🧭

A path to liberation

The Jain path links right faith, right knowledge and right conduct with spiritual progress toward moksha.

♾️

Many-sided truth

Anekantavada encourages people to recognize that reality has many aspects and that a single viewpoint may be incomplete.

⚖️

Karma

Jain philosophy describes karma as subtle matter associated with the soul through actions and passions.

🌱

Careful living

Restraint, compassion and awareness are woven into Jain ideas about daily conduct and spiritual life.

✨

The Tirthankaras

Jains revere 24 Tirthankaras in the present time cycle as ford-makers who revealed the path across samsara.

02 · The heart of the path

Five vows + Three Jewels

Ascetic Jain practice expresses these commitments in their fullest form; lay practice follows corresponding commitments suited to household life.

The five great vows

1
Ahimsa
Non-violence toward living beings.
2
Satya
Truthfulness and careful speech.
3
Asteya
Not taking what is not freely given.
4
Brahmacharya
Celibacy for ascetics and disciplined sexual conduct for householders.
5
Aparigraha
Non-possessiveness and restraint of attachment.

The Three Jewels

Samyak Darshana · Right FaithOrientation toward truth and the teachings.
Samyak Jnana · Right KnowledgeUnderstanding reality without distortion.
Samyak Charitra · Right ConductLiving that understanding through ethical and disciplined action.
03 · A worldview

From careful action to freedom

Jain philosophy teaches that the soul is naturally capable of infinite knowledge, perception, energy and bliss. Karmic bondage obscures these qualities. Spiritual practice is described as stopping new karmic influx and shedding accumulated karma.

05 · Living heritage

Temples, sculpture & memory

Jain heritage survives in architecture, sculpture, manuscripts, pilgrimage landscapes, learning and community life.

🏛️

Ranakpur

Ranakpur in Rajasthan is known for a highly detailed Jain temple dedicated to Adinath (Rishabhanatha), with elaborate stone craftsmanship.
